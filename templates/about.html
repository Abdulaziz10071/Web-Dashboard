About Me

This is what I do.

My professional background bridges the gap between traditional power systems and modern embedded electronics. I have gained hands-on experience in solar PV system design and optimization, calculating output power and payback periods. I have also performed diagnostic testing for industrial transformers, including insulation resistance and full-load tests. Currently, as an Erasmus+ trainee at Vilnius Tech, I am developing an IoT-based monitoring system using Python and Raspberry Pi. This project involves managing dual data streams for real-time processing.

Beyond my technical background, I possess significant experience in international commerce and professional communication. My tenure as a Salesperson and Translator at Ikarde Garden & Home involved managing B2B sales for the global market and serving as the primary linguistic bridge for international stakeholders during complex negotiations. My 18-month commitment as an English Language Instructor at TEOL Language Schools demonstrates that I can simplify complex concepts and maintain professional standards while managing a rigorous academic and technical workload.

I am a trilingual, fluent in English, Arabic, and Turkish, with a proven ability to thrive in diverse environments following my time in Türkiye, Poland, and Lithuania.
